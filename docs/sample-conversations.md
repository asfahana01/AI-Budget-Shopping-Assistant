# Sample conversations

## Conversation A — budget first

User: I have ₹5000.
Assistant: What would you like to buy? For example: backpack, headphones, and a smartwatch.

User: I need a backpack, headphones and a smartwatch.
Assistant: Got it. Do you have any preferences, such as battery life or college use?

User: I prefer good battery life and something suitable for college.
Assistant: Recommends a verified combination under ₹5000 with remaining budget and trade-offs.

## Conversation B — follow-up preference

User: I need headphones under ₹2000.
Assistant: I can look at headphones within ₹2000. Any other products to include?

User: Make them wireless.
Assistant: Filters remaining headphone options to wireless models and keeps the ₹2000 budget.

## Conversation C — image plus chat

User: uploads a backpack photo
Assistant: Stores visual context (type, color, style if visible).

User: Find something similar with headphones and a watch. Budget 5000.
Assistant: Uses visual context as a ranking boost for backpacks, then optimizes the full bundle.
